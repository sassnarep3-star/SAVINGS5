What is not a source here

No comparison site, affiliate site, press article or search-result snippet was used as the basis for any number on this site. Aggregator pages were encountered during research and deliberately excluded. One example: a competitor's calculator quotes Bank of America and Chase savings rates, but neither bank publishes a rate this study could read without a ZIP code, so both cells are blank here even though a number was available from the aggregator.
