# Savings-account research snapshot

**Snapshot / assumed opening date:** 2026-10-22<br>
**Location context:** New York City, NY (approximate; no ZIP code was supplied or inferred)<br>
**Source policy:** Official bank/regulator sources only for decision values. Unsupported APYs and terms are left blank and flagged. All variable-rate projections are scenarios, not forecasts.

## Decision-ready findings

There is no single unconditional winner for every depositor. The result changes with balance, eligibility, promotional timing, and FDIC headroom. For context, the FDIC's broad national savings-account rate was **0.38% as of 2026-10-19**; this is an average across institutions/products, not a high-yield-account peer benchmark. [FDIC October 2026 rates](https://www.fdic.gov/national-rates-and-rate-caps).

- **Highest recent standard tier at a $250,000 starting balance:** Barclays Tiered Savings displays **3.65% APY at $250,000+**, accurate as of 2026-10-07. That is a modeled **$9,125** of first-year interest on $250,000 if the rate holds. The rate source is older than the Oct. 20–21 snapshots below, and $250,000 principal leaves no standard FDIC headroom for accrued interest. [Official Barclays rate and offer page](https://banking.us.barclays/tiered-savings.html).
- **Fresh, uncomplicated alternative:** Synchrony High Yield Savings displays **3.55% APY as of 2026-10-21**, with no minimum deposit/balance or monthly fee. On $250,000, the constant-rate one-year illustration is **$8,875**. [Official Synchrony product/rate page](https://www.synchrony.com/banking/products/hys-core).
- **Conditional offer with the highest currently displayed public rate in this screen:** Barclays Select Savings from Barclays shows **4.25% APY on all tiers** (as of 2026-10-07) for active AARP members. A separate **$400** offer requires a new account, at least $40,000 funded within 30 days, and $40,000 maintained for 120 consecutive days; it expires **2026-10-31**. At $250,000, the rate-held-constant one-year scenario is $10,625 interest plus the conditional $400. [Official AARP/Barclays offer and APY terms](https://banking.us.barclays/select-home/savings.html).
- **SoFi new-member route:** a new SoFi Checking and Savings member can receive a +1.10 percentage-point boost for up to six months, producing a displayed 4.40% APY from the 3.30% qualifying rate (as of 2026-10-20), if eligible activity is met and maintained. SoFi separately advertises up to $400 for qualifying payroll/benefit/government direct deposits. The projections keep the APY boost and cash bonus separate rather than assuming the offers stack. [Official SoFi APY terms](https://www.sofi.com/banking/high-yield-savings-account/).
- **Marcus referral route:** the official referral page offers a +1.00-point APY boost for three months; its standard APY is 3.50% as of 2026-10-21. Use of a Marcus customer referral link is required. [Official Marcus referral offer](https://www.marcus.com/us/en/savings/referrals).
- **CIT BOOST50:** the offer page displays 4.25% APY for six months on $5,000+ balances, then the published 3.75% standard APY. However, the underlying rate disclosure is explicitly dated **2026-07-01**. The scenario is therefore segregated and flagged as stale relative to this 2026-10-22 snapshot, not ranked as a fresh October rate. [Official CIT promotion](https://www.cit.com/cit-bank/platinum-savings/boost) and [official CIT rate disclosure](https://www.cit.com/cit-bank/resources/products-and-rates).

## Offers and account-opening rules

- **Barclays Tiered $200:** new Tiered Savings customers only; previous Barclays savings/CD customers are excluded; open through 2026-10-31; deposit at least $25,000 within 30 days and maintain it for 120 consecutive days; payout about 60 days later. No minimum is required just to open the account. [Official terms](https://banking.us.barclays/tiered-savings.html).
- **Barclays Select/AARP $400:** active AARP membership; new Select Savings customer; open through 2026-10-31; deposit and maintain at least $40,000 under the 30-day/120-day schedule; payout about 60 days later. [Official terms](https://banking.us.barclays/select-home/savings.html).
- **SoFi APY Boost:** first SoFi Checking and Savings account, open during the offer period; within 60 days receive an eligible direct deposit or $5,000 in qualifying deposits, then repeat qualifying activity every 31 days for up to six months. Qualifying deposits are defined inflows; they are not the same as maintaining a $5,000 balance. [Official terms](https://www.sofi.com/banking/high-yield-savings-account/).
- **SoFi direct-deposit cash bonus:** qualifying recurring income direct deposits must total at least $5,000 in the 25-day window for the $400 tier; $1,000–$4,999.99 earns $50. Bank transfers and check/P2P deposits do not count as eligible payroll direct deposits. [Official terms](https://www.sofi.com/banking/high-yield-savings-account/).
- **Marcus referral:** new Online Savings customer must use a current Marcus customer's referral link; +1.00 APY point lasts three months; then-current standard rate applies. [Official terms](https://www.marcus.com/us/en/savings/referrals).
- **CIT BOOST50:** promo code BOOST50 is required at opening/enrollment; offer runs 2026-09-29 through 2026-12-15; new and eligible existing primary customers can qualify; prior bonus/boost and trust/custodial exclusions apply. [Official terms](https://www.cit.com/cit-bank/platinum-savings/boost).
- **TD $200:** qualifying new TD personal savings customer; open online; deposit $10,000 within 20 days and maintain it for 90 additional days after day 20; offer through 2026-11-30; APY remains blank. [Official terms](https://www.td.com/us/en/personal-banking/savings-accounts/featured).
- **SoFi Plus:** official page advertises 4.70% on up to $20,000, 3.30% on remaining savings, and a $10/month membership price. It is modeled separately, with the fee subtracted and no fee waiver or other Plus value assumed; it cannot be combined with the SoFi APY Boost. [Official SoFi Plus terms](https://www.sofi.com/sofi-plus/).

## FDIC coverage and institution identity

The FDIC's standard amount is **$250,000 per depositor, per insured bank, per ownership category**, calculated dollar-for-dollar including principal and accrued/due interest. Same-bank deposits in the same ownership category aggregate. [FDIC FAQ](https://www.fdic.gov/resources/deposit-insurance/faq).

- A $250,000 starting principal is already at the limit before interest. The projected interest on that amount is not automatically insured.
- Count checking, savings, CDs, and other deposit balances held at the same legal bank and in the same ownership category.
- CIT Bank is a division of First Citizens Bank & Trust Company; CIT states those division deposits are not separately insured. [CIT FDIC information](https://www.cit.com/cit-bank/platinum-savings).
- SoFi Savings automatically opens a SoFi Checking account; both are SoFi Bank deposits for standard-limit aggregation. [SoFi account terms](https://www.sofi.com/banking/high-yield-savings-account/).
- Barclays Tiered and AARP Select are Barclays Bank Delaware deposits and do not create separate bank limits. [Barclays FAQs](https://banking.us.barclays/faq.html).
- SoFi's additional up-to-$3M coverage is a separate optional Insured Deposit Program, not standard direct-bank insurance; it is not included here. [Official program terms](https://www.sofi.com/banking/fdic/sidpterms/).
- Use [FDIC BankFind](https://banks.data.fdic.gov/bankfind-suite/bankfind) to confirm the legal bank and [EDIE](https://edie.fdic.gov/) for a specific coverage calculation. FDIC coverage protects insured deposits against bank failure, not losses due to theft or fraud ([FDIC brochure](https://www.fdic.gov/resources/deposit-insurance/brochures/deposits-at-a-glance)).

## Open issues published to Flagged for Review

1. NYC ZIP-specific rates for legacy banks were not obtained; no local ZIP was invented.
2. CIT's explicit rate disclosure is dated 2026-07-01, so its displayed figures are not treated as fresh for the snapshot.
3. PNC's official product title/body conflict; availability and APY stay unresolved.
4. Marcus has an older 2017 limit amendment; the current 2025 agreement and FAQ agree on the current $3 million aggregate/$1 million per-account limits. The older wording is retained as a resolved historical discrepancy, not used for current calculations.
5. Capital One has a separate `NaN` extraction path; the explicit 3.20% table/effective-date disclosure is used and the application screen should be rechecked.
6. The official SoFi Insured Deposit Program is separate from standard FDIC coverage and is not assumed.
7. All savings APYs/offers are variable and may change; re-open the official source on the day of application.
