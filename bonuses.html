This records what the official account page did or did not show on the date checked. It is not a claim that the bank has no offer anywhere, and it is not a claim about targeted or invitation-only mailers.

A bonus is not a rate. A $200 bonus on a $10,000 deposit held for 90 days is worth roughly 0.8% annualized over that quarter — meaningful, but it does not change which account is best for a multi-year balance. The return tables treat bonuses as separate from APY and never add them into a projection.
