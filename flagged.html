What was left blank, and why that is the right call

6 of the 17 accounts have no numeric APY anywhere on this site. In each case the bank either requires a ZIP code, serves an empty table, or renders a placeholder. The FDIC publishes a national savings average of 0.38%; plugging that into the four mega-bank rows would have made the table look complete and would have been an invention. Their fee, minimum and rule data — which is published — is still included, because it is verifiable.
