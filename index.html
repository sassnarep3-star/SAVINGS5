Key Strategic Findings

  • A 424-basis-point chasm separates national direct HYSAs from branch megabanks. On $250,000 opened 2026-10-22, Barclays Select Savings pays $10,625/yr (4.25%), CIT Bank Platinum with BOOST50 pays $9,999 in Year 1 (4.00% blended; 3.75% ongoing), and Synchrony pays $8,875/yr unconditionally (3.55%) — versus $25/yr (0.01%) on Chase Savings, Wells Fargo Way2Save, and Truist One Savings. Barclays Chase NY PDF
  • Depositing exact $250,000.00 at one bank leaves Year-1 accrued interest uninsured for a single owner. FDIC coverage caps principal + unpaid accrued interest at $250,000 per charter. At 4.25% APY, depositing $239,808.15 compounds to exact $250,000.00 at Year 1 with zero uninsured spillover — or titling as a 2-owner Joint account ($500,000/bank) or 5-beneficiary POD Trust ($1,250,000/bank) protects the entire principal plus multi-year compounding inside just 1–2 banks. FDIC rules
  • Shared bank charters create hidden FDIC aggregation traps across divisions & mergers. Discover Bank merged into Capital One (CERT 4297) on May 18, 2025; CIT Bank is a division of First-Citizens Bank (CERT 11063); and BMO Alto is a web-only division of BMO Bank N.A. (CERT 16571) with no mobile app. Every multi-bank ladder on this site deduplicates strictly by FDIC Certificate number. FDIC API
  • Liquid savings beat the FDIC national average by 11.2×, yet trail U.S. Treasuries by 27–88 bps. Following the Federal Reserve's September 17, 2026 rate hike to 3.75%–4.00%, 1-year and 5-year U.S. Treasury par yields sit at 4.52% and 4.95% (2026-10-21) — the exact liquidity premium you pay for instant penalty-free access.
