Methodology and verification

How every number on this site was obtained, checked and published — and the rules that decided what was left blank.

Scope

  • Large national U.S. banks offering consumer savings to customers nationwide. Small and local community banks and credit unions were excluded by request.
  • Every institution had to be FDIC-insured, confirmed against FDIC BankFind by certificate number — not by the bank's own claim.
  • A consumer mobile app was a requirement. Where it could not be confirmed from an official page the field was left blank and flagged, not assumed.
  • 18 institutions and 23 deposit products are published, of which 18 have a verified headline APY.

The verification rule

  1. A value is published only if it was read directly from the institution's own website, rate sheet or disclosure PDF, or from a .gov source.
  2. If a value could not be read, the cell is left blank, marked unverified, and written up on Flagged for Review with the link needed to finish the check.
  3. Nothing is inferred, estimated, averaged, or carried over from a previous month.
  4. Aggregator sites (Bankrate, NerdWallet, Forbes Advisor, Investopedia and the like) were never used as a source of record. Web search was used only to locate official URLs; the value was then read off the official page itself.
  5. Where two official sources disagreed, both are published and the disagreement is flagged — see F-09, F-10 and F-11.
  6. Marketing comparisons published by one bank about another bank are treated as marketing, not as data. Each bank's rate always comes from that bank.

The return model

ending balance = principal × (1 + APY)days / 365  ·  interest = ending balance − principal

  • Account opened 2026-10-22 (UTC).
  • APY held constant for the whole horizon. Every savings APY here is variable.
  • No further deposits, no withdrawals, interest left on deposit.
  • No fees deducted; fee terms are published per account.
  • 365-day year in the exponent; calendar maturity dates do account for leap days.
  • For tiered accounts the APY applied is the one for the band that the deposit falls into, not the advertised headline.
  • Projections are marked as derived. They are not sourced, because they are arithmetic performed on sourced inputs.

Build-time guards

The site is generated from JSON by scripts/build.py. Nothing is hand-typed into the HTML. The build fails if:

  • any APY used by the return calculator is not present in the balance-tier table captured from that bank;
  • any record marked unverified still carries a value;
  • any published product lacks a source URL;
  • any internal link on the site points at a page that does not exist.

All four guards passed for this build. Generated 2026-10-22T00:00:00Z.

Known limits of this capture

  • Rates were captured at one moment in time, 22 October 2026, 00:00 UTC, and every savings APY here is variable.
  • Several branch banks set rates by ZIP code and the capture tool cannot hold their location cookie. Each affected row states the location the figure actually applies to.
  • Citi, TD and Citizens publish their APYs only through client-side rendering that did not resolve; those figures are blank, not guessed.
  • This is a research summary, not financial advice, and not a solicitation on behalf of any institution.

Reproducing this

Clone the repository and run python3 scripts/build.py. The data lives in data/*.json; the schema and verification contract are in data/SCHEMA.md; the task board, source log and irregularity log are in COORDINATION.md.
