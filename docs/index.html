What this research answers

  • Every account, every rate, with the bank's own effective date on each row.
  • What you actually earn at six deposit sizes across seven holding periods, with maturity dates.
  • How to hold the maximum insured amount and what it costs you to spread money around.
  • Which opening bonuses are real, what you must deposit and how long you must keep it there.
  • The rules for opening each account — minimums, fees, waivers, withdrawal limits, eligibility.
  • Whether a rate is actually competitive, measured against the FDIC's published national rates.

How to read this site

Every row carries a source↗ link to the bank's own page, rate sheet or disclosure PDF, or to a .gov source. Nothing comes from a comparison site.

A blank cell with unverified means the official page exists but the number could not be read — usually because it is behind a ZIP-code gate or rendered only by JavaScript. Those cells are deliberately empty. Each one is written up on Flagged for Review with the link you need to finish the check yourself.
