Return on the size of your deposit

What each account actually pays, by deposit size and holding period. Every projection assumes the account is opened 22 October 2026 — today — with interest left on deposit and the APY held constant. Accounts whose rate could not be verified are excluded from these tables rather than estimated.

Read this before you use the numbers

Formula: ending balance = principal × (1 + APY)days/365. Interest shown is ending balance minus principal.

  • Account-opening date: 22 October 2026. Each maturity date below is that date plus the stated number of days.
  • Every one of these APYs is variable. Holding it constant for two, three or five years will not happen in practice — treat the long horizons as a ranking tool, not a forecast.
  • No fees are deducted and no further deposits are made. Fee terms are on Opening rules.
  • For tiered accounts the APY used is the one that applies to that deposit size, not the headline rate.
  • Where a bank caps the balance, larger deposits are shown as over cap instead of a made-up number.
