Is the Barclays bonus worth it? The arithmetic

The Barclays $200 is the only pure cash bonus on a savings account found on an official page. It needs $25,000 funded within 30 days and held for 120 days.

  • Interest on $25,000 at 3.50% for 120 days: $284.36
  • Plus the bonus: $200
  • Total over the 120-day qualifying window: $484.36
  • Annualised, that is an effective 6.01% for the qualifying period — versus 3.50% without the bonus.

Offer expires 31 Oct 2026, so the account must be opened within 9 days of 22 October 2026. The bonus is paid roughly 60 days after the 120-day hold, i.e. about 20 April 2027 if funded immediately, and Barclays treats it as interest for tax purposes (Form 1099-INT). Full terms, as published

Barclays — Tiered Savings: Cash bonus — savings account source↗

Who qualifies: New Barclays Tiered Savings customers only. Current and previous Barclays customers with a Savings or CD are not eligible.

When it pays: Approximately 60 days after the 120-day holding period is met

  • Open the account through 10/31/2026.
  • Fund the new Tiered Savings account with at least $25,000 within the first 30 days of opening.
  • Maintain a balance of at least $25,000 for 120 consecutive days.
  • The $200 bonus is added to the account approximately 60 days after that.
  • The bonus is applied and treated as interest for tax purposes and may trigger a Form 1099-INT.
  • Multiple deposits from other institutions may be combined, provided all occur no later than 30 days after opening.
SoFi — SoFi Savings: Promotional APY — savings account source↗

Who qualifies: Open your first SoFi Checking and Savings account by 12/31/27 and set up eligible direct deposit within 60 days.

When it pays: Boost applies for up to 6 months while the eligible direct deposit is maintained

  • 1.10% APY boost is added on top of the standard savings APY (3.30% as of 10/20/26), giving up to 4.40% APY.
  • Boost lasts up to 6 months as long as the eligible direct deposit is maintained.
  • If the variable APY changes, the 1.10% boost still applies on top of the then-current rate.
  • Opening a SoFi Savings account automatically opens a SoFi Checking account.
SoFi — SoFi Checking and Savings: Cash bonus — requires direct deposit source↗

Who qualifies: Open the account and set up eligible direct deposit of $1,000 or more.

When it pays: Not stated on the page captured

  • Earn a $50 or $400 cash bonus with eligible direct deposit.
  • Also unlocks getting paid up to two days early.
  • Full terms are published by SoFi at sofi.com/banking under footnote 4.
Wells Fargo — Platinum Savings: Promotional APY — savings account source↗

Who qualifies: New Wells Fargo savings customers only.

When it pays: Rate applies to balances of $25,000 or more; the standard APY (0.01%) applies below that

  • Deposit and maintain $25,000 or more in a new Platinum Savings account within 30 days.
  • Rate is variable and may change.
  • $12 monthly service fee applies unless a $3,500 minimum daily balance is kept each fee period.
  • $25 minimum opening deposit.

Rate location: CA 94105 (the location wellsfargo.com defaulted to)

Marcus by Goldman Sachs — Online Savings Account: Promotional APY — referral source↗

Who qualifies: Requires a Marcus referral.

When it pays: 3-month promotional APY increase

  • 3-month promotional APY increase of 1.00% on top of the stated Online Savings Account APY.
  • After 3 months the account earns the then-current standard APY.
  • Marcus states that with one referral in a year, assuming the standard APY is unchanged, the account would earn a combined APY of 3.75% for that year.

Full terms: https://www.marcus.com/us/en/savings/referrals#termsandconditions↗
