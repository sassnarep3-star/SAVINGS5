F-01 high

Citi publishes no readable savings APY

Institution
Citibank, N.A. (FDIC cert 7213)
Field affected
Citi Savings — APY, minimum deposit, monthly fee
What happened
Every official Citi rates URL tried returned either an empty JavaScript shell (citi.com/banking/current-interest-rates/savings-accounts), a 'page not found' redirect (online.citi.com/US/ag/banking/checking-savings-account-interest-rates, citi.com/banking/savings-accounts), or failed to load (preview.online.citi.com). Citi requires a state selection before rendering rates.
What this site did
All Citi values left blank and marked unverified. No Citi number is published anywhere on this site.
How you can resolve it
Open the official Citi rates page in a browser, select your state, and read the Citi Savings / Citi Accelerate Savings rows.

F-15 medium

Expanded FDIC coverage through partner-bank networks is a program feature, not a higher FDIC limit

Institution
SoFi Bank, N.A.
Field affected
FDIC coverage up to $3,000,000
What happened
SoFi advertises 'additional FDIC-insurance up to $3M through a network of participating banks'. The FDIC's own limit is $250,000 per depositor, per insured bank, per ownership category. Coverage above that is achieved by sweeping balances to other insured banks under a program agreement, so it depends on the program working as described and on you not already holding deposits at the receiving banks.
What this site did
SoFi's $3M figure is published with this distinction stated on the row and explained on the FDIC page.
How you can resolve it
Read SoFi's footnotes 6 and 7 and the participating-bank list, and check whether you already bank with any receiving institution.
Sources to check
  • SoFi — High Yield Savings (official)↗
  • FDIC — Understanding Deposit Insurance↗
