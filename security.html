What the FDIC actually covers

Ownership categoryFDIC ruleLimit shownSource
Single accountAll single accounts owned by the same person at the same bank are added together and insured up to $250,000.$250,000source
Joint account (2 owners)Each co-owner's shares of every joint account at the same insured bank are added together and insured up to $250,000 per co-owner.$500,000source
Certain retirement accounts (e.g., IRA)All retirement accounts listed by the FDIC owned by the same person at the same bank are added together and insured up to $250,000.$250,000source
Trust account (revocable / irrevocable)FDIC formula: # of owners x # of beneficiaries x $250,000, not to exceed $1,250,000 per owner for all trust accounts.$1,250,000source
Employee benefit plan accountEach participant's non-contingent interest is insured up to $250,000 per bank; for contingent interests (e.g., health and welfare plans) coverage is $250,000 for the plan itself.$250,000source

Not covered: stocks, bonds, mutual funds, annuities, life insurance, municipal securities, crypto assets, U.S. Treasury securities, and the contents of safe deposit boxes. FDIC list

Security was not independently audited — and is flagged for your review. This project confirms three things: the institution is FDIC-insured, the charter type, and whether an app is named on the official page. It does not verify encryption standards, multi-factor authentication, fraud-reimbursement policies, or app-store ratings, and it makes no comparative security ranking. Treat any security claim about these banks, including claims implied by a polished app, as unverified until you read the bank's own security page.

An irregularity worth knowing about: Discover

Discover Bank (FDIC certificate 5649) is returned by the FDIC BankFind API with ACTIVE = 0. Capital One's own savings page states that Discover Bank deposit accounts opened on or after May 18, 2025 are counted together with Capital One deposit accounts for FDIC coverage.
