1. Compounding & Promotional Blending Formulas

  • Constant-APY future value: FV = P × (1 + APY)t where t is measured from 2026-10-22 (0.25, 0.50, 1, 2, 3, 5, 10 years).
  • CIT Bank BOOST50 Year-1 blended APY (≥$5,000): 1.04250.5 × 1.03750.5 - 1 = 3.9997% (≈ 4.00%).
  • SoFi 6-Month Direct-Deposit Boost Year-1 blended APY: 1.0440.5 × 1.0330.5 - 1 = 3.8485% (≈ 3.85%).
  • Marcus 3-Month Referral Boost Year-1 APY: 3.75% combined Year-1 APY per Marcus's official disclosure (up to $1,000,000 balance cap).
  • Day-1 Single-Owner Principal Buffer Cap: Pmax = ⌊$250,000 / (1 + APYYr1)⌋ so principal plus full Year-1 compounded interest never exceed $250,000.00.
  • 5-Year Rate-Cut Stress Case: Year 1 at today's verified effective yield, Years 2–5 at min(ongoing APY, 0.38% FDIC National Average).
